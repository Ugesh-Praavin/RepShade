## 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Repshade is a mobile workout tracking application designed to make strength-training progression simple, fast, and consistent.

Unlike traditional workout trackers that tie workouts to specific weekdays, Repshade follows the user's actual training sequence.

If a user completes:

Push → Pull → Legs

and misses two days after Push, Repshade does not automatically move them to Legs.

Their next workout remains:

Pull

The app remembers where the user is in their training program and continues from there.

### Core Product Statement

Repshade helps you train consistently, track every set, and keep progressing without being controlled by the calendar.

---

## 2. THE PROBLEM

Most people do not train perfectly according to a calendar.

A typical workout program might be:

Monday — Push
Tuesday — Pull
Wednesday — Legs
Thursday — Rest
Friday — Push

Real life may look like:

Monday — Push ✓
Tuesday — No workout
Wednesday — No workout
Thursday — Pull
Friday — No workout
Saturday — Legs

Traditional workout apps often treat the calendar as the source of truth.

This creates several problems.

### Problem 1 — Missed Workouts

Missing a day should not mean the workout disappears.

### Problem 2 — Poor Workout Memory

Users often do not remember:

* What weight they used
* How many reps they completed
* How many sets they did
* Whether they improved from their previous session

### Problem 3 — Slow Logging

Many workout trackers require too many interactions to record a simple set such as:

22.5 kg × 10 reps

### Problem 4 — Unclear Progression

Users record workouts but do not always understand whether they are actually improving.

### Problem 5 — Calendar Guilt

Missing a workout can make an app feel like a task manager rather than a training companion.

---

## 3. PRODUCT OPPORTUNITY

Repshade should turn workout tracking into a simple loop:

TRAIN
↓
LOG
↓
REMEMBER
↓
PROGRESS
↓
TRAIN AGAIN

The app should remove everything unnecessary between those steps.

## 4. PRODUCT VISION

Build the simplest training companion that helps people consistently execute their workout program and see measurable progress over time.

Repshade should eventually become the user's:

* Digital gym notebook
* Workout history
* Progress tracker
* Lightweight progression assistant

---

## 5. PRODUCT MISSION

Make every workout count.

Every set should become useful data.

Every completed workout should move the user forward.

Every future workout should benefit from what the user did previously.

---

## 6. CORE DIFFERENTIATOR — THE ROLLING SPLIT

This is the defining feature of Repshade.

Traditional workout apps follow:

DATE → WORKOUT

Repshade follows:

COMPLETED WORKOUT → NEXT WORKOUT

Example:

PUSH
↓
Completed
↓
PULL
↓
Completed
↓
LEGS
↓
Completed
↓
PUSH

The calendar records when something happened.

The split determines what happens next.

### Core Product Principle

The calendar tells you when you trained.

Your split tells you what you train next.

This principle must influence the product architecture, UX, copywriting, and marketing.

## 7. TARGET USERS

### Primary Persona — Consistent but Not Perfect Gym User

Typical characteristics:

* Trains 3–6 times per week
* Follows a workout split
* Wants to gain strength and/or muscle
* Does not always train on fixed days
* Wants to track weights and reps
* Wants to see progression
* Does not necessarily need complicated coaching

### User Mindset

"I know what workouts I should do. I just need an easy way to track them and make sure I'm progressing."

Repshade should not assume that every user needs to be taught how to work out.

## 11. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

### Principle 1 — Training Comes First

The fastest path should always be:

Open → Start → Log → Finish

---

### Principle 2 — Never Punish Missed Days

If the user does not train, the program waits.

Do not show messages such as:

"You failed your streak."

Instead show:

"Your next workout is Pull."

---

### Principle 3 — Previous Data Should Always Be Useful

When performing an exercise, the user should immediately see previous performance.

Example:

LAST SESSION

22.5 kg × 10
22.5 kg × 9
20 kg × 10

The app should help answer:

"What should I do today?"

---

### Principle 4 — User Controls the Program

Repshade can suggest:

"Try 25 kg today."

But it should never silently change the user's workout program.

---

### Principle 5 — Logging Must Be Fast

Recording a set should require as few interactions as possible.

---

### Principle 6 — Data Should Work Offline

Poor internet connectivity should never prevent someone from logging their workout.

---

### Principle 7 — Do Not Over-Gamify

PRs and progression are naturally motivating.

The app does not need:

* XP
* Coins
* Fake rewards
* Excessive badges
* Competitive leaderboards
